Before you send a report

Include the details below when relevant. This helps us reproduce the issue.

  • Your device model, operating system, and TalkToMe app version.
  • What you expected, what happened, and the steps to reproduce it.
  • For AI issues: the model name, file size or quantization, and any error message.
  • For purchase issues: the product name, store, and date of purchase.
  • A screenshot or short recording, with private information removed.

Never send passwords, authentication tokens, payment card details, or unredacted receipts. You do not need to send your private conversation history.

Quick answers

A model will not load, or responses are slow

Available memory, model architecture, file size, quantization, and generation settings affect compatibility and speed. Try a smaller compatible model and close other memory-intensive apps. Not every GGUF architecture is supported. Send the model name and exact error if the problem continues.

Voice input is not working

Check microphone and speech-recognition permissions in your device settings. Speech availability depends on your operating system, language, and installed resources; some speech services require an internet connection.

A purchase is missing

Check that you are using the store account that made the purchase, then use the app's purchase-restoration option for eligible non-consumable purchases. Cookies are consumable items and are handled differently. If access or a cookie purchase is still missing, contact support with the product name and purchase date. Do not buy the same item again just to troubleshoot.

I want to report an AI response

AI responses can be inaccurate or inappropriate. Send the model name and a brief description of the issue. Share only an anonymized excerpt if it is necessary; do not include sensitive information. Verify important information independently.
